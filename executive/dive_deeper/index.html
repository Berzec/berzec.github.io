Dive Deeper into the Executive Branch!

An overview of the Executive Branch

Overview

The SGA Executive Branch is the body within SGA that has executive powers. Here is where many of SGA's biggest faces are. The Executive Branch is split into four distinct groups: SGA Officers, the SGA Presidential Cabinet, SGA Class Officers, and SGA Honor Court Officers.

SGA Officers

SGA Officers ensure SGA operations run smoothly, especially regarding special events, Senate, and Honor Court matters. There are three SGA Officer positions: SGA President, SGA Vice President, and SGA Secretary. All three positions are elected positions.

The SGA President's main task is to preside over SGA broadly. Powers vested with the President include, but are not limited to, appointing a Presidential Cabinet, writing and passing Executive Orders, presenting and executing Senate legislation, and calling special meeting sessions with any body of SGA.

The SGA Vice President's main task is to preside over the SGA Senate. Powers vested with the Vice President include, but are not limited to, leading all Senate meetings, casting tie-breaking votes, appointing Senate-specific positions, and presenting Senate legislation to MSMS administration. The Vice President also serves as the backup President.

The SGA Secretary's main task is to keep the SGA organized. Powers vested with the Secretary include, but are not limited to, organizing Senate legislation, assisting the Presidential Cabinet in their duties, presenting Senate legislation to MSMS administration, and maintaining the SGA OneDrive.

Presidential Cabinet

The Presidential Cabinet assists the SGA President in their duties. The Presidential Cabinet may contain however many Secretaries the SGA President sees fit; however, permanent positions within the Cabinet include Class Officers Liaison, Press Secretary, and Secretary of Digital Affairs. All positions within the Presidential Cabinet are appointed.

The Class Officers Liaison's main task is to organize MSMS traditions. The Liaison is given special powers to accomplish this, including the ability to present bills before the Senate and attendance at Junior Class Officer and Senior Class Officer meetings.

The Press Secretary's main task is to promote SGA events and the Senate through journalism and social media. The Press Secretary is given special privileges to accomplish this, including access to SGA affiliated social media accounts and automatic affiliation with journalistic organizations.

The Secretary of Digital Affairs's main task is to maintain the SGA website. The website must include information regarding the Senate, legislation updates, member names, and a copy of the Constitution. The SODA is given the ability to design the website to their discretion, given approval by the SGA President.

Class Officers

The Class Officers' organize class events and activites throughout the school year. There are 10 total officers, 5 for each class. Major events that Class Officers may organize include, but are not limited to, Prom, Graduation, and class gifts. The 5 positions per class are President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Historian. All Class Officer positions are elected.

Senior and Junior Class Officers share similar tasks. Both organize major events and activities for their respective class. However, Senior Class Officers are also tasked with organizing a class gift project, assisting with orientation activities, assisting with graduation activities, and assisting with class reunions. Junior Class Officers are tasked with organizing Prom.

Similarly, Senior and Junior Class President share similar tasks. Both are tasked with organizing activities for their respective class, leading Class Officer meetings, and examining and approving budgets. However, Senior Class Presidents are also tasked with organizing class reunions, presenting a class gift project to MSMS administration, and requesting a graduation speaker.

Both Senior and Junior Class Vice Presidents share the same task. Both are tasked with personally assisting the Class President with their duties, as well as assisting other officers when necessary.

Both Senior and Junior Class Secretaries share the same task. Both are tasked with keeping accurate minutes of Class Officer meetings, assisting other officers when necessary, personally assisting the Class President with their duties, and maintaining their respective class's OneDrive archives.

Senior and Junior Class Historians share similar tasks. Both are tasked with keeping accurate records of class activities. However, the Senior Class Historian is also tasked with serving as the Yearbook President, as well as creating a Class slideshow that accurately reflects the Class's experiences at MSMS.

Honor Court Officers

Honor Court Officers preside over the SGA Honor Court. They ensure the fair selection of judges, assist MSMS administration in justice-related matters, and review constitutionality among SGA members. There are three Honor Court Officer positions: Honor Court Chief Justice and two Honor Court Associate Justices. All Honor Court Officer positions are appointed.

The Honor Court Chief Justice's main task is to lead the Honor Court. Powers vested with the Honor Court Chief Justice include, but are not limited to, appointing judges, writing decision rationale for Honor Court cases, assisting the Director for Student Affairs in Honor Court matters, reviewing constitutionality of SGA members, and maintaining the Honor Court OneDrive archives.

The Honor Court Associate Justice's main task is to assist the Honor Court Chief Justice. Powers vested with Honor Court Associate Justices include, but are not limited to, reviewing Honor Court applications, writing dicision rationale for Honor Court cases, and reviewing constitutionality of SGA members.
