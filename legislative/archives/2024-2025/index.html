September 2024
SB1 Morgan Smith

To allow for students with large gaps in their schedule to leave campus for lunch.

To make the first offense of an unexcused absence be a Level One Exception and to lessen the punishments of each offense.

SB3 Jacob Heisler

To provide clarity on when students are allowed to complete dorm hall work service and provide students with more opportunities to complete dorm hall work service.

SB4 Ruth Albright

To change the requirement of having no Cs for O Plan to being able to have one C and to change the requirement of having no Cs for Premium Plan to being able to have two Cs.

To give students a little leeway involving the grading portions of privilege plans.
