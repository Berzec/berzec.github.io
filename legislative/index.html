The Legislative Branch Mission

MSMS SGA's Legislative Branch is dedicated towards proposing new ideas and changes that benefit the MSMS community. From rule amendments to staff recognition, the Legislative Branch will be advocating for change, ensuring that MSMS will be ready for the present and future.

Watch Senate Meetings!

Sit with your representatives by joining Senate meetings. Senate meetings are open to the public and occur every Wednesday at 6PM in Hooper Auditorium.

I want something to change

Do you have something you want to see changed at MSMS? Maybe it's a policy that you really dislike, or a rule you think should be updated? Find a Senator and tell them! Let them know about the change you wish to see and they'll conjure up a bill about it. Or, submit your idea using this form. We'll make sure your ideas get passed along to the right person!
