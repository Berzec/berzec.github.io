Senate meetings will begin next Wednesday on 2 September 2026 at 6PM in Hooper Auditorium. All students are welcome to watch Senate meetings. SGA congratulates each elected Senator and wishes them the best of luck in the upcoming legislative sessions.

Want to participate in student affairs but didn't get elected as Senator? Don't worry! Special elections will be held for any empty Senator slots at the first Senate meeting. Be on the lookout for the application email as we do have empty slots!
